## 🎮 One Home for Your Games

**ARCANE** is a modern, highly customizable Windows gaming hub engineered to unite your PC titles, retro collections, and emulated games into a single, immersive ecosystem.

| 🕹️ DESKTOP MODE | 🎬 BIG PICTURE | 🎨 YOUR STYLE |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Clean, high-density library for everyday desktop gaming. | Full controller navigation designed for TV & couch sessions. | Theme Studio, custom grid artwork, and dynamic covers. |

---

## ✨ Core Features & Highlights

* **SmartScan** — Automatically indexes your directories for supported PC & emulator executables.
* **SmartCover** — Automatically fetches high-resolution box art and banners for a clean grid layout.
* **Emulator Integration** — Seamless launch integration with retro emulation engines.
* **Theme Studio & Gallery** — Personalize your launcher’s theme, colors, and layout elements.
* **Pocket Nova** — Discover and play lightweight embedded HTML games and companion tools.
* **Torrent & Repack Workflows** — Built-in automation tools in supported builds.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

1. **Download** the official `ARCANE 0.7.0` release package directly from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hadibader/Arcane/releases/tag/Arcane0.7.0).
2. **Extract & Run** the installer, then follow the setup wizard.
3. **Configure** your game directories, ROM folders, and emulator pathways in the settings panel.

> ⚠️ **Important Safety Reminder:**
> Always download build binaries directly from official release pages. If SmartScreen or your antivirus raises a unknown publisher notice, verify the source before proceeding.
